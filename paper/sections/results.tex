The exact Stage-6 ledger records four supported conclusions: direct pseudo-supervision, uncertainty/reliability, online audio input modality, and sample-specific pseudo alignment under its negative control. It records seven unsupported contribution claims: sparse MTL joint training, task-aware fusion, semantic evidence via depression pseudo acceptance, both RA-STCH claims, video modality contribution, and fusion amplification of corpus decodability.


\subsection{Contextual video, text, and DEPART comparison}
Table~\ref{tab:context} separates WSM evidence from external DEPART results. The accepted V2 prototype-aware video reference has DEV D/P/Mean 0.620101/0.793043/0.706572. T1 is text-only context, not additive fusion evidence. Published DEPART is strong for visual-only depression recognition; our audio and A+V systems are particularly strong on Parkinson, while fusion mainly improves depression relative to audio and can reduce Parkinson relative to audio. This is contextual rather than a strict leaderboard comparison: DEPART's article metrics are best compared on UAR, its manually cleaned Test is not automatically identical to TEST\_HARD, and our primary metric is Mean\_Score.
