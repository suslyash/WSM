\section{Limitations}

The partial-label setting is a central limitation, not merely a training detail. We do not verify correctness of missing disease labels, recover true comorbidity, or claim that pseudo labels are clinically correct. The observed label mask prevents unobserved labels from becoming negatives, but it does not replace dual annotation. The work is not a clinical deployment claim.

The primary role of shared fusion is a frozen DEV-only parsimony decision, not a Final-Test superiority result. All predeclared paired final-Test Mean intervals include zero, and no post-Test model, threshold, or role revision is permitted. The DEPART comparison is contextual because target formulations, reported metrics, and cleaning protocols differ. T1 is standalone evidence and does not establish an additive A+V benefit. T2 was blocked before model evaluation, so it cannot support any description-family performance claim.

Finally, corpus identity is structurally coupled to the observed task in these data. The corpus probe did not trigger its strong-decodability flag, but this does not establish absence or causality of a corpus shortcut. Unsupported component outcomes are similarly narrow and do not establish universal uselessness. No further experiment is authorized by the current programme.
