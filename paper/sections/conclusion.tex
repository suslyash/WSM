\section{Conclusion}

WSM records a frozen evidence programme for multimodal depression and Parkinson classification with partial labels. The final paper candidate is equal-parameter shared fusion, selected before Test reporting by a DEV-only parsimony decision; full R4 and temporal audio remain the frozen multimodal reference and baseline. One-shot final Test reporting preserves these roles and does not establish statistical superiority. The ablation record supports direct pseudo-supervision, reliability handling, the online audio branch, and a narrow sample-specific alignment control, while preserving several negative mechanism results. The resulting manuscript is intentionally conservative: it reports observed-task generalization evidence, not verified recovery of unknown diagnoses, comorbidity, or clinical truth.
